About

Double X

9th Grade · Largo High School · Class of 2029

Xxavier “Double X” Grant is a 9th grader at Largo High School who already carries the résumé of an upperclassman. A lockdown defender and dominant rebounder on the basketball court, a relentless competitor in the outfield, and a six-instrument performer with leadership responsibilities in the Largo Lions Marching Band — Double X doesn't choose a lane. He owns all of them.

His vision is the NBA. His model is Kobe Bryant. His standard is everything — on the court, on the field, and on the stage. At 14 years old, Xxavier Grant is just getting started, and every program he touches gets better because he's in it.

Off the court and stage, Xxavier also operates as DJ Double X, bringing the same intensity and creativity he applies to athletics to the art of music production and performance.

Why Double X?

You're not recruiting a position. You're recruiting a competitor. Xxavier “Double X” Grant brings the defensive intensity and toughness that programs build championship cultures around.
